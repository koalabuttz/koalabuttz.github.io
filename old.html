I like making computers do things they were never supposed to do.
Sometimes that means putting Tailscale on a PlayStation Vita. Sometimes
it means squeezing a Rust roguelike onto a Commodore 64. Usually it means
disappearing down a rabbit hole until the weird idea actually works.





Professionally, I've done systems administration across Windows, Linux,
and macOS — servers, identity, virtualization, monitoring,
networking, automation, and all the documentation that keeps everything
from becoming folklore.





I tend to bounce between systems, software, security, and weird
hardware. “Technical generalist” is the term I use to
describe myself. I like learning unfamiliar systems, fixing what's
broken, and automating the annoying parts. Right now I'm looking for
systems administration, IT/infrastructure, or junior software
engineering work where that kind of curiosity is useful.





There's a collection of things I've built below. Welcome to my website.
I'm glad you're here.

  projects

tailscale-vita — a Tailscale client for the PlayStation Vita

  
>github.com/koalabuttz/tailscale-vita

  
>rust · wireguard · noise ik · derp · stun · vita · MIT

  

  A Rust implementation of the Tailscale protocol stack for Vita:
  Noise IK control, DERP relays, Disco path discovery, a WireGuard data
  plane, ACL enforcement, and direct-path NAT traversal. A Vita on Wi-Fi
  reached a phone on cellular over a 68 ms direct UDP path.
  






  
standard-reader (sr) — a terminal reader for standard.site long-form writing on the AT Protocol

  
>try the web reader · github.com/koalabuttz/standard-reader

  
>rust · tui · wasm · atproto oauth · offline-first · GPL-3.0

  

  A cross-platform, offline-first reader with terminal and browser/WASM
  frontends. It handles six publishing formats, full-text search, images,
  OAuth-backed subscriptions, and prebuilt releases for Linux, macOS, and
  Windows over one portable Rust core.
  






  
bsky-vita — a Bluesky (AT Protocol) client for the PS Vita

  
>github.com/koalabuttz/bsky-vita

  
>rust · at protocol · oauth · hardware video · homebrew · GPL-3.0

  

  Browse feeds, post image threads with alt text, watch color video with
  hardware H.264 decoding, send DMs, and sign in through OAuth
  (PAR/PKCE/DPoP) or an app password. It ships as one installable VPK.
  






  
roguelike — a cross-platform dungeon crawler, from modern terminals to the Commodore 64

  
>github.com/koalabuttz/roguelike

  
>rust · no_std · ssh · mcp · c64 · gba · nds · GPL-3.0

  

  One portable rules core scales from modern terminals down to the C64,
  GBA, and Nintendo DS. It includes deterministic dungeon generation,
  an SSH server, native console builds, and an MCP interface that lets
  an AI play headlessly.
